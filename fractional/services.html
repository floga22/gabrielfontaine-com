Services

Three ways to bring senior product leadership into your team.

Every engagement is scoped around a concrete outcome, not a monthly retainer for open-ended hours. Pick the shape that matches where you're stuck.

01 · Retained

Fractional Head of Product

Embedded 2–3 days per week as your acting product leader: roadmap, team and stakeholder management included.

  • Roadmap ownership & prioritization
  • Team mentoring & hiring support
  • Board & executive reporting
  • Cross-functional stakeholder alignment

Ideal for teams between hires

02 · Project

Strategy & Roadmap Sprint

A focused 2–4 week engagement that turns a fuzzy backlog, or an unresolved regulatory requirement, into a prioritized, fundable roadmap.

  • Vendor & stakeholder evaluation
  • Prioritization framework & scoring
  • 12-month roadmap deliverable
  • Executive readout & working session

Ideal for a specific decision

03 · Interim

Interim Leadership Coverage

Full-scope coverage during a leadership transition, extended leave or active executive search.

  • Full-time-equivalent coverage
  • Executive search support & handoff
  • No dropped roadmap commitments
  • Structured onboarding for the successor

Ideal for leadership gaps

FAQ

Common questions.

How is this different from a consultant?

A consultant typically hands you a recommendation and moves on. This means being embedded: sitting in standups, reviewing real specs and carrying the same roadmap accountability a full-time leader would, for the length of the engagement.

What size company is this for?

Companies in identity, fraud, risk or compliance-adjacent spaces, from growth-stage teams to regulated enterprise divisions, that need senior product judgment without a full headcount commitment.

How much time does an engagement take?

Anywhere from a focused 2–4 week sprint to an ongoing 2–3 day-per-week retainer, scoped in writing before anything starts.

What does it cost?

Pricing depends on scope and cadence, discussed openly on the discovery call, with no surprises in the proposal.
