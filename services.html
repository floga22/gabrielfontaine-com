FAQ

Common questions.

How is this different from a consultant?

A consultant typically hands you a recommendation and moves on. This means being embedded — sitting in standups, reviewing real specs, and carrying the same roadmap accountability a full-time leader would, for the length of the engagement.

What size company is this for?

Companies in identity, fraud, risk, or compliance-adjacent spaces — from growth-stage teams to regulated enterprise divisions — that need senior product judgment without a full headcount commitment.

How much time does an engagement take?

Anywhere from a focused 2–4 week sprint to an ongoing 2–3 day-per-week retainer — scoped in writing before anything starts.

What does it cost?

Pricing depends on scope and cadence — discussed openly on the discovery call, no surprises in the proposal.

Is Gabriel only doing fractional work, or also considering full-time roles?

Both, in parallel. Several companies he's talked to have proposed a fractional or interim arrangement as a lower-commitment starting point before — or instead of — a full-time hire, which is exactly the shape these engagements take.

Not sure which engagement fits?

That's exactly what the discovery call is for — no commitment, just clarity on the right shape.
