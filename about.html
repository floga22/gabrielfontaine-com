About

Gabriel Fontaine

Product Management Leader · Identity, Fraud & Compliance

Alpharetta, GA · Bilingual English/Spanish · LinkedIn

I'm a senior product management executive with 18+ years of experience, the last 11+ spent inside the identity, fraud, and compliance data industry at LexisNexis Risk Solutions and Equifax.

I operate at the intersection of three things that don't often coexist in one person: deep regulatory fluency (FCRA, GLBA, KYC, KYB, AML), hands-on technical range (I build prototypes, test vendor SDKs, and integrate APIs with GenAI tools), and large-scale organizational leadership (I've run a 45-person cross-functional Agile organization from funding ask through shipped product).

I have managed and built product teams from scratch under my direct supervision — Product Managers, UI/UX, Rules Editors, and Business Analysts — while coaching Engineering, Data Science, and Data Stewards and working closely with Legal, Compliance, Privacy, Fraud, Marketing, and Finance on cost containment, margin improvement, and pricing strategy.

My consistent pattern is turning regulatory constraints into monetizable, API-delivered products — CIP, KYC, and KYB requirements are the starting spec, not the ceiling. Every compliance product I've shipped carries pricing, go-to-market, and a revenue or pipeline number attached.
